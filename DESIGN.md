## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of gourmet confectionery and high-performance nutrition. The visual language conveys warmth, handcrafted luxury, and uncompromising wholesomeness. It resolves the tension between fitness discipline and dessert indulgence by presenting nutrition as an exquisite, sensory pleasure rather than a clinical restriction.

The target audience comprises health-conscious gourmands, dedicated fitness enthusiasts, and lifestyle-oriented consumers who demand clean ingredient decks (sweetened by whole dates, high bio-available protein, zero artificial preservatives) without sacrificing velvety mouthfeel and artisanal decadence.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic
The interface draws from **Neo-Artisan Confectionery** combined with **Tactile Heritage Luxury**:
- **Warm, organic tactile surfaces:** Deep cocoa velvety cards, warm ivory parchment planes, and textured borders that recall patisserie stamp badges and letterpress packaging.
- **Cobalt badge accenting:** Drawing directly from the heritage insignia, royal cobalt blue provides an unexpected, authoritative contrast against rich chocolate tones.
- **Fine copper & gold gilding:** Hairline metallic trims and subtle warm highlights elevate functional nutritional metrics into badges of artisanal quality.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural chocolate-making, antique pastry workshop ceramics, and royal insignia:

- **Primary (`#2B160C` - Warm Roasted Cocoa):** Used for primary headings, high-impact branding structures, primary interactive surfaces, and deep anchor framing. It evokes 85% single-origin dark chocolate.
- **Secondary (`#0F3F96` - Royal Cobalt Blue):** Directly inspired by the heritage seal, this shade is used for primary conversion buttons, certified purity badges, protein stat highlights, and critical link states.
- **Tertiary (`#C9873E` - Warm Amber Toffee / Burnished Copper):** Serves as an accent for star ratings, nutrient callout ribbons, metallic borders, and subtle interactive glows.
- **Neutral (`#FBF7F0` - Chantilly Cream):** The warm, non-glare canvas that replaces sterile digital white, providing an organic background reminiscent of fresh dairy cream and natural date flesh.

### Functional Roles & Contrast Rules
- Surface default sits on `#FBF7F0`, with card elevation shifting subtly to `#FFFDF9`.
- Dark contrast blocks (such as decadent hero sections and footer banners) utilize `#2B160C` surfaces with `#FBF7F0` text and `#C9873E` hairline borders.
- Functional success colors leverage an earthy sage (`#3E6B48`), while alert/caution is anchored by natural dried persimmon (`#B84A28`).
- High-protein metric chips utilize Cobalt-on-Cream (`#0F3F96` text on `#EBF1FA` background) or inverse Cobalt ribbons with bold white text.

## Typography

The typography unites two worlds: the culinary heritage of European pastry salons and the clean readability expected of modern health products.

- **Display & Headings (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serifs provide the gravitas, indulgence, and classical patisserie elegance needed for brand slogans and dessert tier naming.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern, geometric, yet gently rounded letterforms provide flawless legibility across ingredient lists, macro nutritional tables, and pricing badges.

### Typography Guidelines
- Macro stat numbers (e.g., "12g PROTEIN", "54g") utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` in bold or extra-bold to retain a modern fitness identity without looking clinical.
- Slogans and emotional brand promises ("Rich. Creamy. Irresistible.") should be rendered in uppercase or title-case serif with generous line spacing.
- Sub-labels detailing clean facts ("Zero Added Sugar", "Sweetened With Dates") must use uppercase `label-badge` with generous tracking to reinforce artisan stamp motifs.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a harmonious 8pt spatial grid with half-step intervals (4px / `0.25rem`) for fine-grained metric badges and nutrition pill alignment.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive fluid grid, `margin-desktop` (48px), `gutter-desktop` (24px). Max container width capped at 1280px to preserve editorial intimacy.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, 32px margins, 20px gutters. Dual-card arrangements for product sizes (e.g., 100g Single Cup vs. 450g Tub).
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `margin-mobile` (20px), `gutter-mobile` (16px). Nutrition badges reflow from horizontal strips into 2x2 grids or compact carousel sliders.

### Spacing Philosophy
Editorial whitespace mimics luxury food journals: dense, tightly engineered nutrition data cards are offset by generous breathing room around visual product shots, cacao powder textures, and signature ingredient callouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained through warm, tactile ambient shadows and layered tonal containers rather than stark digital blurs.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Cream (`#FBF7F0`), completely flat.
2. **Level 1 (Gourmet Cards):** Pale Cream (`#FFFDF9`) surrounded by a 1px border of `#EADDC9` (Warm Biscuit). Subtle ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 22, 12, 0.05)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Floating Modals):** Crisp elevated surface with dual shadow structure: `0 10px 30px -4px rgba(43, 22, 12, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(43, 22, 12, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Signature Product Floats & Badges):** Distinctive deep chocolate cast shadow with warm amber bleeding: `0 16px 40px -6px rgba(43, 22, 12, 0.16)`.

### Decorative Tactility
- **Artisan Borders:** Key callouts employ double concentric hairline borders (an outer 1px solid line in `#0F3F96`, a 2px offset space, and an inner 0.5px hairline), mirroring the circular vintage lid badge.
- **Warm Overlays:** Dark container sections feature a radial gradient bloom originating from `#3F2213` falling off into `#1E0F08`.

## Shapes

The shape system blends the rounded softness of gourmet whipped mousse with the structured discipline of high-end packaged goods. A roundedness factor of `2` (medium-high) gives elements friendly, approachable edges without descending into juvenile or cartoonish territory.

- **Micro elements (Badges, Pills, Macro Tags):** `rounded-full` (pill shape) to evoke smooth, scooped spoonfuls and fitness nutrition tags.
- **Interactive Buttons:** `rounded-lg` (16px) or fully rounded pill styles for primary actions.
- **Product & Recipe Cards:** `rounded-xl` (24px) to convey softness and warmth.
- **Circular Seals:** Circular geometry (`rounded-full`) is reserved for the artisan stamp logo, protein percentage stamps, and ingredient iconography circles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Artisan Blue CTA):** Background `#0F3F96`, text `#FBF7F0`, font `title-sm` with letter-spacing `0.02em`. Padding `14px 28px`. Hover elevates with `box-shadow: 0 6px 20px rgba(15, 63, 150, 0.3)` and subtle background tint to `#0C3278`.
- **Secondary Action (Decadent Cocoa):** Background `#2B160C`, text `#FBF7F0`, hover tint to `#412314`.
- **Tertiary (Artisan Outline):** Background transparent, 1.5px solid border in `#0F3F96` or `#2B160C`, text matching border color.
- **Pill Pack Button (Add to Cart / Weight Selector):** Compact pill toggle displaying weight (e.g., "100g Cup" vs "450g Tub") with smooth border switch and subtle amber accent highlight when selected.

### Product & Nutrition Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in `#FFFDF9` with a delicate 1px biscuit border (`#EADDC9`). Top section features a photography viewport framed with soft inner corner rounding; bottom section hosts the macro badge rack.
- **Macro Badges Bar:** A horizontal container displaying four standardized icons:
  1. High Protein count in bold Cobalt (`#0F3F96`).
  2. Date sweetener icon with warm brown badge.
  3. "Zero Added Sugar" slash badge.
  4. Handcrafted badge with subtle heart glyph.

### Chips & Seals
- **Artisan Stamp Seal:** A circular badge bordered by dual concentric rings containing curved text or bold inner nutrition markers (e.g., "12g PROTEIN / ZERO ADDED SUGAR"). Background `#FBF7F0` with text and ringwork rendered in `#0F3F96`.
- **Dietary Filter Chips:** Pill shapes with neutral cream fill (`#F4ECE1`), transitioning on active selection to solid cobalt fill (`#0F3F96`) with crisp white typography.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height 48px, background `#FFFDF9`, 1px border in `#DBC8B2`. On focus: 1.5px border in `#0F3F96` accompanied by an ambient ring `rgba(15, 63, 150, 0.12)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted feel with rounded 4px corners on checkboxes and circular radio wells. Active fill `#0F3F96` with a custom ivory checkmark/bullet.

### Flavor & Ingredient Callout Ribbons
- **Banner Ribbons:** Deep cobalt or burnished copper banners featuring outward-facing swallowtail cuts or gold-stitched borders. Centered typography in `Playfair Display` or uppercase `label-badge` with heart or floral ornament anchors.
